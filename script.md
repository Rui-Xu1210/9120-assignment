Script

This video demonstrates our vaccination tracker and log application, designed to help parents track their children’s vaccination schedules, reminders and records in one place.

To start, click “Set up your family”. Enter the parent’s name, 
then click “Save and continue”. Next, enter the child’s name and date of birth, choose a parent, and save the child’s profile. 

++Schedule++

Parents can manage more than one child by clicking “Add a child” on the Family page.

On the Schedule page, all the vaccines grouped by recommended age are shown. Each entry shows the vaccine name, dose number and planned date. 

Click on a vaccine name to view its details, including the planned date, vaccination status, provider, vaccine type and diseases prevented.

After the vaccination has been given, enter the actual vaccination date and provider, then save the record. 
The system marks the corresponding schedule and reminder as completed, keeping the linked information consistent.

To add a schedule, click on a vaccine name. Then, click “Change planned date” to enter the planned vaccination date. By default, the app calculates this date using the child’s date of birth and the recommended vaccination age stored in the database. 
Parents can change it to match their planned appointment.

++History++

Next is the History page. 
All the child’s vaccination records are displayed here, including the vaccine name, dose number and vaccination date.

We can now see the record we just saved. 
Parents can click on an entry to view its details or select “Add record” to enter another vaccination record.

++Reminders++

Finally, the Reminders page allows parents to view Upcoming, Completed or All reminders. 

Each entry shows the vaccine name, dose number, reminder date and time.

Click on an Upcoming reminder to view its planned vaccination date and customise the reminder date and time. 

Under Completed, we can see the reminder linked to the vaccination we recorded earlier.

The application connects to PostgreSQL to store and retrieve this information.
